# Settings

In the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K; Option-K on a Mac), under **Far Ground**:

* **Far ground** (on): off, the game's own ground to the horizon, as the game draws it.
* **Points apart** (3 px): how far apart its points are on the screen, 2 to 6 pixels. 2 is the finest and costs the graphics
  card about twice as much as 3; 6 is the coarsest and the cheapest.
* **The game's own ground within** (6 km): nearer the craft than this the game's own ground is drawn, as the game draws it;
  beyond it, the far ground. Further: more of the game's own quads, which costs the processor more.

The page also says what it is drawing (how many points, from how far, how far the game's own ground reaches) or why it is not.
